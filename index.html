FAQs

When can clients invest after the NFO closes?

An open-ended fund reopens for regular buying a few days after the NFO closes. The reopening date is in the scheme document.

Is a ₹10 NFO unit cheaper than an existing fund?

No. The unit price does not make a fund cheap or expensive. What matters is the strategy, the fund manager, the costs and fit with the client's goals.

Who can invest in a SIF?

Specialised investment funds need a minimum investment of ₹10 lakh per investor across SIF strategies of a fund house, as set by SEBI.

Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. Read all scheme related documents carefully before investing.
